Common iPhone & Samsung Troubleshooting Solutions

1. Battery Draining Quickly (iPhone & Samsung)

  • Cause: Background app refresh, high screen brightness, or battery degradation.
  • Fix: Go to Settings > Battery > Enable Power Saving Mode. Turn off "Background App Refresh" for non-essential apps.

2. Overheating During Usage or Charging

  • Cause: Heavy gaming, fast charging in hot environments, or rogue background processes.
  • Fix: Remove heavy protective cases while charging. Force restart the device and update to the latest iOS/Android version.

3. Wi-Fi / Cellular Signal Drops

  • Cause: Corrupted network configuration settings.
  • Fix: Toggle Airplane mode ON for 10 seconds. If unresolved, go to Settings > General / System > Reset Network Settings.

4. Screen Blackout or Frozen Display

  • Fix for iPhone: Press & quick release Volume Up, then Volume Down, then hold Side Power Button until the Apple logo appears.
  • Fix for Samsung: Press and hold Power Button + Volume Down simultaneously for 10-15 seconds to force reboot.
